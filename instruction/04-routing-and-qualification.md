# Qualification, Routing, and Result Pages

## Internal scoring

Score 0–2 on each dimension:

- Cost of problem
- Urgency
- Decision authority
- Evidence availability
- Cross-functional complexity
- Prior failed attempts
- Implementation capacity
- Investment readiness
- Willingness to test assumptions

### Tier A

High cost, authority, urgency and implementation capacity. Show assessment booking.

### Tier B

Meaningful problem but missing evidence, alignment or budget. Manual review and smaller diagnostic recommendation.

### Tier C

Researching, no material consequence, wants free consulting or refuses to examine assumptions. Education route.

### Regulated route

Legal, medical, financial, safety or other regulated matters require appropriate qualified professionals. Variety Portal can route business-system aspects without substituting for regulated advice.

## Booking page

# Bring the Problem. Leave the Preferred Solution at the Door.

Prepare:

- What changed
- The expected result
- What happened instead
- What has already been tried
- Relevant data
- Decision-makers
- Cost of waiting
- Constraints on time, budget and implementation

Do not send passwords, confidential customer data, privileged legal documents or regulated personal information.
