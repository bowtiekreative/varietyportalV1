## Preliminary result routes

### Evidence gap

# You May Need Better Evidence Before a New Solution.

Recommend measurement, research or a smaller test.

### Clear specialist route

# Your Problem Appears Ready for a Specialist Review.

Recommend the relevant affiliated or external provider with disclosure.

### Ambiguous multi-system route

# The Symptom Has Multiple Plausible Causes.

Recommend the paid 216-Path Assessment.

### Urgent route

# The Cost of Waiting Requires Human Review.

Route to priority consultation without making an automated conclusion.
