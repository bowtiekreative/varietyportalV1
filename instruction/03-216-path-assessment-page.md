The 216-Path Business Assessment decomposes the problem, challenges the current diagnosis, compares alternative interventions and recommends the smallest action likely to produce useful evidence.

## When to use it

- Multiple providers recommend different solutions
- Previous attempts failed or produced temporary results
- The problem crosses brand, marketing, sales, operations or technology
- The next decision is expensive or difficult to reverse
- The team cannot agree on the cause
- The business is buying activity without progress
- You need to know what should happen first

- Intervention comparison

- Smallest sufficient intervention

- Stop conditions and review date

## Assessment process

### Scope

Define the question, decision, stakeholders, evidence and constraints.

### Decompose

Map objects, conditions, actions, tools, resources, dependencies and outcomes.

### Generate

Use morphological variety to expose paths the current framing excludes.

### Filter

Remove paths that fail evidence, feasibility, risk, cost, time or strategic-fit tests.

### Recommend

Select a first move and explain why it precedes the alternatives.

### Route

Define the internal owner, affiliated company or outside specialist required.
