## Protocol

### 1. Describe the symptom

Tell us what is happening, what you expected and what it is costing.

### 2. Decompose the system

We separate the people, objects, conditions, actions, tools, resources, dependencies and outcomes.

### 3. Generate variety

The 6×6×6 engine explores 216 alternative states and paths.

### 4. Eliminate poor fits

Options are tested against evidence, urgency, cost, reversibility, capacity and strategic fit.

### 5. Prescribe the first move

The recommendation explains what should happen, why it comes first and what evidence should result.

### 6. Route the work

When implementation is needed, we recommend an affiliated company, a coordinated combination or an outside specialist.

## Symptom categories

### “We need more leads.”

Possible constraints include positioning, offer, visibility, proof, conversion, follow-up, sales capacity or measurement.

### “Our website is not working.”

Possible constraints include traffic quality, messaging, local relevance, accessibility, trust, user journey or sales response.

### “We need AI or automation.”

Possible constraints include process design, information quality, ownership, exception handling or choosing a task that should not be automated.

### “The team keeps dropping the ball.”

Possible constraints include unclear decisions, missing resources, fragile handoffs, incentives, capacity or a process with no alternative path.

### “Our brand needs a refresh.”

Possible constraints include identity, category, audience, promise, proof, experience or implementation inconsistency.

### “We do not know what to do next.”

That is exactly what Variety Portal is designed to route.

## FAQ

### Is Variety Portal a marketplace?

Not initially. It is a diagnostic and routing service. You are not asked to browse hundreds of profiles or accept competing bids before the problem is understood.

### Do you always recommend one of your own companies?

No. Affiliated companies are recommended only when the analysis supports the fit, and the relationship is disclosed. The correct route may be an outside specialist, an internal action or no purchase.

### What does 216 paths mean?

The engine combines six interrogatives, six relational modifiers and six quantitative scales to challenge assumptions and generate alternative ways to understand or resolve a problem.

### Is the engine fully automated?

No. The engine expands the possibility space; human judgment reviews evidence, feasibility, risk and context.

### What if I already know what I need?

Use the scan to confirm the route. Clear, evidence-backed needs can move directly to the appropriate provider.
